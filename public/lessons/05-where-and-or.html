Combining conditions: AND, OR

Build richer filters with AND, OR and parentheses.

The idea

AND needs both sides to be true. OR needs at least one. When you mix them, use parentheses — AND binds tighter than OR, so without brackets the meaning changes.

Here: electronics (category_id = 1) that are either expensive or running low.

What to watch

  • Products that are not electronics fall first, then electronics that are neither pricey nor low in stock.
Careful. Try removing the parentheses and watch the result change — a classic source of bugs.
