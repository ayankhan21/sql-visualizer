Sorting with ORDER BY

Put the rows in a meaningful order.

The idea

Without ORDER BY a database may return rows in any order. ORDER BY price DESC sorts from the highest price down; ASC (the default) goes low to high.

Sorting happens near the end, after filtering and after the SELECT list is computed.

What to watch

  • Watch the chips physically slide past each other into their sorted positions.
Tip. Sort by several columns: ORDER BY category_id, price DESC.
