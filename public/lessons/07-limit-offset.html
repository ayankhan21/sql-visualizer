Top-N with LIMIT and OFFSET

Take just a slice of the sorted rows.

The idea

LIMIT 5 keeps at most five rows. OFFSET 2 first skips two. Together they page through results: "page 2 of the price list".

LIMIT only makes sense after ORDER BY — otherwise "the first five" is arbitrary.

What to watch

  • After sorting, the first two rows and everything beyond row seven are cut away.
