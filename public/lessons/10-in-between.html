IN and BETWEEN

Shorter ways to write common filters.

The idea

x IN (2, 5) is shorthand for x = 2 OR x = 5. x BETWEEN 15 AND 40 means x >= 15 AND x <= 40 — both ends are included.

What to watch

  • Books (2) and toys (5) survive the first test; then the price range removes the rest.
