SUM, AVG, MIN and MAX

More ways to summarise a column.

The idea

The classic aggregates: SUM adds, AVG averages, MIN / MAX pick the extremes. NULLs are ignored. With no GROUP BY, the whole table is one group, so you get exactly one row.
