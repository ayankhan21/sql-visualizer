Removing rows with DELETE

Take rows out of a table.

The idea

DELETE FROM table WHERE … removes matching rows. No WHERE means all rows go.

In a real database, deleting an order that other tables (like order_items) still point to would be blocked or cascade — this playground does not enforce that, so you can experiment freely.

What to watch

  • Cancelled orders are marked, then physically drop out of the table.
