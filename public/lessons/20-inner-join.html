INNER JOIN — connecting tables

Follow a foreign key to combine two tables.

The idea

Data is split across tables to avoid repeating it. An order stores only customer_id — a foreign key pointing at customers.id. A JOIN glues related rows back together.

ON o.customer_id = c.id is the matching rule: a customer row and an order row are paired when this is true. c and o are table aliases.

What to watch

  • Pink customer chips and red order chips land side by side.
  • Each matching pair merges into one wide row that carries both colours.
  • Customers without any order have nothing to pair with — INNER JOIN drops them.
Tip. The colours tell you where every cell came from: pink = customers, red = orders.
