LEFT JOIN — keep everything on the left

Do not lose rows that have no partner.

The idea

An INNER JOIN silently drops rows with no match. A LEFT JOIN keeps every row of the left table; where the right side has no partner its columns are filled with NULL.

What to watch

  • Two customers (Sam Carter, Tara Brooks) have no orders. They stay in the result, with NULL in the order column.
