Finding what is missing (anti-join)

LEFT JOIN + IS NULL = "rows with no match".

The idea

A very common question: which customers never ordered? Do a LEFT JOIN, then keep only rows where the right side came back NULL. Those are exactly the unmatched left rows.

What to watch

  • Step 1 builds the LEFT JOIN (with NULLs for non-buyers). Step 2: WHERE throws away everyone who did match.
Tip. The same idea answers "products never sold": products LEFT JOIN order_items … WHERE order_items.id IS NULL.
