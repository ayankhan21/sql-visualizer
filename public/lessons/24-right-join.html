RIGHT JOIN — keep everything on the right

Mirror image of LEFT JOIN.

The idea

RIGHT JOIN keeps every row of the right table. Here that is departments — so a department with no employees (Legal) still appears, with a NULL employee.

Most people just swap the table order and use LEFT JOIN; both are equivalent.

What to watch

  • Watch Legal stay behind with NULLs while all other departments pair up with their employees.
