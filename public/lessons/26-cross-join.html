CROSS JOIN — every combination

Pair every row with every row.

The idea

A CROSS JOIN has no ON condition. Each of the 6 categories is paired with each of the 6 suppliers: 6 × 6 = 36 rows. It is the raw material of every other join — a join is a cross join plus a filter.

What to watch

  • The result chips multiply: each category chip fans out into six.
Careful. Accidental cross joins (a forgotten ON) are the #1 cause of "my query returned millions of rows".
