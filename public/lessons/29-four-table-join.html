Joining four tables

Follow the chain order_items → orders / products → categories.

The idea

Big questions usually span many tables. The trick is to read the foreign keys like a map: order_items points at orders and products; products points at categories.

Filter early (WHERE oi.quantity >= 3 could also be written in the first ON) — fewer rows to carry through each join.

What to watch

  • The row grows wider with each join: orange items, then red order, green product, lime category cells.
Tip. Open the Analyze tab to see how the row count changes after each join.
