JOIN + GROUP BY

Count related rows per entity.

The idea

Join first, group second. The LEFT JOIN guarantees every customer is present; COUNT(o.id) counts only real orders (it skips NULL), so customers without orders correctly show 0.

What to watch

  • Customers with no orders form a bucket whose COUNT(o.id) is 0 — with COUNT(*) they would wrongly show 1.
Careful. Use COUNT(o.id) not COUNT(*) after a LEFT JOIN, otherwise the NULL row is counted.
