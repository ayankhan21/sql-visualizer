EXISTS and NOT EXISTS

Ask "is there at least one…?"

The idea

EXISTS (subquery) is true when the subquery returns at least one row. What it selects does not matter — SELECT 1 is a convention. Use NOT EXISTS for "has none".

What to watch

  • Customers with no cancelled order are dropped.
Tip. Change it to NOT EXISTS to find customers who never cancelled.
