LAG and LEAD — peek at neighbours

Compare a row with the one before or after it.

The idea

LAG(x) looks one row back in the window order; LEAD(x) one row forward. The first/last rows have no neighbour, so they get NULL. Handy for "change since last period".

What to watch

  • The cheapest product has NULL for next_cheaper; the priciest has NULL for next_pricier.
