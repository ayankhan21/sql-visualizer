Putting it all together

CTEs + joins + grouping + window ranking in one report.

The idea

A realistic report: who are our top spenders?

  1. CTE 1 totals every order.
  2. CTE 2 joins customers → orders → totals and sums per customer.
  3. The final query ranks customers with a window function and keeps the top eight.
